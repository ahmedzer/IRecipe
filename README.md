# IRecipe

An Android app for **recipe discovery**, **ingredient-based suggestions**, and **maintenance management** of a connected dispenser. Designed for users who love experimenting with recipes and need a smart way to manage their kitchen or bar setup.

---

## 📱 Features

### 🌍 Explore Recipes
Discover an extensive and diverse collection of recipes — from refreshing drinks to hearty meals. Unlock exciting new flavors and culinary ideas with ease.

### 📖 Step-by-Step Instructions
Follow clear, detailed, and beginner-friendly instructions for each recipe. Whether mixing or cooking, you’ll be guided every step of the way.

### 🕓 Recipe History
Track your culinary journey by automatically saving a history of all recipes you've prepared. Revisit your past creations whenever you want.

### ❤️ Favorite Recipes
Easily save and organize the recipes you love most, so you can quickly recreate them whenever you feel like it.

### 📸 Ingredient Recognition
Take a photo of your available ingredients. The app uses AI to identify them and instantly suggests recipes you can prepare using what you have.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Recipe Search
Find the most relevant and creative recipes using simple keywords or ideas. Backed by **Google Cloud Gemini**, the search adapts to your style and preferences.

## 🧠 What I Learned

- ✅ Built with **Clean Architecture**: separation into Data, Domain, and Presentation layers.
- ✅ Used **Dagger Hilt** for Dependency Injection, promoting modular and testable code.
- ✅ Integrated **Retrofit** for API calls with **Gson/Moshi** for JSON parsing.
- ✅ Implemented **image recognition** to detect ingredients using the camera and AI models.
- ✅ Developed advanced recipe search using **Google Cloud Gemini API**.
- ✅ Managed persistent local storage using **Room** and **Kotlin Flows**.
